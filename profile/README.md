##  Who We Are

**Cletrix Labs** is an open-source organization focused on building practical AI tools for students globally.

We believe every student - regardless of language, country, or background - deserves access to powerful AI that helps them learn faster, research smarter, and grow further.

We build with **Python, AI Agents, LangChain, and Gemini API** - and everything we make is free and open source. 💙
